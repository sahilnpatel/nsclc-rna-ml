# Credit and reuse

Sahil Patel carried out the original high-school study and supplied the research
files. The archived student/mentor manuscript lists Sahil Patel as the student
and Madhav Subramanian as the mentor. The study was conducted with iResearch
mentorship. The later, unfinished ML work involved a Vanderbilt collaborator;
that person's name and contribution have not yet been added because they have
not been confirmed.

The repositories include new AI-assisted reconstruction and analysis code from
November 2026. Original scripts and notebooks are kept separately in `archive/`.
They should not be confused with the newer implementation.

Public availability does not grant a blanket license to every file. The paper,
presentation, third-party gene sets, and TCGA-derived data retain their applicable
rights and source terms. No blanket open-source license has been assigned.
Cite the original study and the relevant data/method sources when using this work;
consult those sources' terms before redistributing their materials.

The large expression matrix, R workspace, and obsolete serialized model are not
included. Source hashes and original filenames are recorded in `source_manifest.json`.
